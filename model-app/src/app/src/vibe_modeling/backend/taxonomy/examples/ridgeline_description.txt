Ridgeline Energy is a fictional regional, investor-owned electric and gas utility serving about 1.9 million electric and 850,000 natural gas customers across a three-state Midwest territory under state public utility commission (PUC) regulation. It owns transmission, distribution, and a small regulated generation fleet, and buys the rest of its supply under power purchase agreements and in the MISO wholesale market. It runs two businesses the model must keep distinct: Ridgeline Electric (generation, transmission, distribution, and growing distributed energy resources such as rooftop solar and batteries) and Ridgeline Gas (pipeline, regulator stations, and service lines under PHMSA integrity rules). Customers are residential, commercial, and industrial, on regulated tariffs, budget billing, and income-qualified programs. This is Ridgeline's enterprise data model: a source-agnostic silver layer that must conform exactly to Ridgeline's own published domain taxonomy of ten domains: customer, billing, grid, gas, asset, outage, supply, finance, workforce, and safety. Do not rename, merge, split, drop, or invent domains. Premise and service point are the backbone the rest of the model hangs off: meters, outages, grid and gas assets, and bills all connect to the service point.
